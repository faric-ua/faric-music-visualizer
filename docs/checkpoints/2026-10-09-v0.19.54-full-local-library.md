## Completed feature batch

1. Home has nine functional local-media sections: All Tracks, Folders, Albums, Artists, Genres, Years, Favorites, Recently Played, My Playlists.
2. Albums/artists/folders/genres/years group real MediaStore audio metadata; click group to open its song queue, tap a track to play via the existing Media3 PlaybackController.
3. Search works across track, artist, album, folder, genre and year. Sort modes: title, artist, album track order, date added, duration.
4. Persistent local hearts/favorites; app playback transitions including queue Next and Previous create Recent entries. Recently means **played in FARIC**, not all external phone app history.
5. My Playlists supports create, add/remove, delete and play. Saved locally in app SharedPreferences as URI references. Deleting a playlist does not delete device files.
6. Browser category, group and query state persists across Android screen recreation.
7. Home displays device-local track, album and artist counts; media scanning off UI with cache, manual refresh and legacy genre fallback.
8. Preserved scene host/player architecture; changes touch no Hero art, no immutable center calibration, no export engine.
