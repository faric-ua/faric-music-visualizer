# v0.4.0 Phone Test

## Playback
- [ ] Start local track.
- [ ] Lock screen for at least 60 seconds.
- [ ] Confirm audio continues.
- [ ] Unlock and confirm position advanced normally.
- [ ] Press Home and confirm audio continues.

## projectM LAB
- [ ] Open Now Playing.
- [ ] Tap "projectM".
- [ ] projectM LAB opens fullscreen.
- [ ] Visual preset is visible (not black).
- [ ] Visual reacts to the currently playing audio.
- [ ] Tap screen at least 3 times and confirm preset changes.
- [ ] Back returns to PulseDeck and audio never restarts/seeks.
- [ ] Record FPS/heat impression after 2 minutes.

## Stop conditions
- black screen;
- native crash;
- audio stops when entering/leaving LAB;
- severe stutter or device heating.
